Header/footer and product/service/industry detail links now use actual catalog routes; unfinished screens clearly say their content is being prepared. The arrows above still describe **future content/conversion intent**, not completed page content or working product/demo access. See the [route inventory](website-structure.md#local-preview-route-inventory). Intentional homepage capability anchors and memory-only project-brief actions remain available.

The production journey requires approved contact/demo access, server validation/spam protection, lawful storage/consent, reliable delivery and clear response states. ## Validation gate

Before treating these as confirmed personas, obtain owner-approved audience priorities and genuine customer evidence. Until then, use them only to organize report-driven content and questions; do not publish “research shows” or fabricate quotes/testimonials.
