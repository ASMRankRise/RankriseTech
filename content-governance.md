## Publication rules

- Cite source-derived recommendations. Label every inferred persona/role and name interpretation **INFERRED**; no official etymology, mission/history or “customer research” invented from them.
- Do not fabricate dashboards, product UI, testimonials, client logos, achievements, statistics, certifications, awards, customer counts, team members or delivery outcomes. Do not borrow other repositories' assets or legal/contact facts.
- Screenshots must be **authentic and approved**; mask customer/personal information, credentials, tokens and confidential identifiers. Record current product/version and image rights; abstract SVG/CSS is illustration, not UI evidence.
- Validate all live product claims, pricing, routes, access/security/support and technical proficiency. A potential industry use case does not establish previous delivery, certification or regulatory compliance.
- Case studies accurately state challenge, role/scope, architecture and verified approved outcomes; anonymous/confidential work still needs permission. No fake three-to-six-project gallery.
- Editorial content requires genuine authorship/date/rights/review. Do not present the three insight shells as published articles, invent authors/dates or fabricate an editorial history.
- Keep product branding distinct but related to the parent. Approved naming and current architecture prevail over unconfirmed module suggestions; do not imply support for every technology candidate.
- Legal pages must reflect actual data collection and jurisdictions. Do not paste boilerplate from another business, publish invented office/contact facts or enable analytics/form collection before required privacy/consent decisions.

## Inquiry, privacy and security — public-launch gates, not setup now

No approved email, phone, address or inquiry backend is supplied. Initial interaction must clearly state **inquiry delivery is unavailable** and provide honest information, not pretend submission works. Do not collect/retain/send real personal information without approved infrastructure/policy; do not silently discard it or show fake success.

Before public launch, require all of:

- Approved intended CRM/email destination and operational owner; accurate response expectations, contact details and product/demo routing.
- **Server-side validation** of input, accessible labels/errors, spam protection/abuse controls and secure transport/routing.
- Approved secure storage/access, retention and sensitive-data handling; privacy notice and consent/cookie controls as actually required.
- Implemented success/error/unavailable states that reflect actual backend outcomes; no success before confirmed acceptance/delivery handling.
- End-to-end delivery verification to the intended destination, failure handling and security review. UI validation alone is not reliable inquiry delivery.
- Tested CTA/form analytics only with approved consent/privacy configuration; no unapproved tracking or external accounts now.
